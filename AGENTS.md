## Contributor Guidance

This entry point governs development of this repository. It is not the
consumer harness entry point. Read relevant knowledge before changing the
area it describes, and maintain that knowledge when durable understanding
changes.

When writing Markdown, follow
[Markdown Writing](harness/documents/markdown.md).
When performing source-control operations, follow
[Source Control](harness/source-control/GUIDANCE.md).
When changing Go code, follow [Go Guidance](harness/go/GUIDANCE.md) and its
applicable linked guidance.

These references reuse specific consumer guidance for contributor work;
they do not activate the entire consumer harness or its workflows.
